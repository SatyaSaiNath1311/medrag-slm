### Key Lessons Incorporated into MedRAG-SLM Design

1. **Honest Reporting of Non-Wins**: In published benchmarks (e.g. MedRGB, Rare-Case Clinical Retrieval), medical fine-tuned models do not uniformly beat generic baselines. Our design commits to reporting trade-offs honestly across all research questions (RQ1–RQ4).
2. **Standardized Pipeline Conditions**: RAG pipeline components (MedCPT embeddings, FAISS IVF-PQ index, BM25 fallback, top-$k=5$, context budget 1,800 tokens) are held completely invariant across both generic and medical models.
3. **Controlled Thinking Caps**: For reasoning models (`MedPsy` and `Qwen3-Thinking`), the reasoning cap is strictly fixed to 1,024 tokens to isolate domain specialization from reasoning length advantages.
4. **Precision-Safe Execution**: Based on empirical literature and logs, Gemma-based models (Gemma-3-4B, MedGemma-1.5-4B-IT) use `float32` execution on Kaggle T4 to avoid fp16 underflow/overflow.
